# 05 — User Personas

> Journey Maps doc.

## Persona template

For each persona:

- **Name / role**
- **Context** — what situation are they in when they encounter this product?
- **Goals** — what are they trying to accomplish?
- **Pain points** — what's broken or frustrating today?
- **Success criteria** — how do they know the product worked for them?

## Persona 01 — The High-Achieving Woman (primary)

**Context.** Age 30–55; business owner, executive, manager, consultant,
healthcare professional, lawyer, entrepreneur. Highly responsible, high
performer, intelligent, emotionally aware, constantly "holding everything
together," usually successful on the outside. She rarely says "I'm
struggling" — she says "I'm just tired," "I can't switch off," "I don't
know why I feel like this," "I should be grateful."

**Pain points.** Burnout, perfectionism, people-pleasing, hypervigilance,
relationship struggles, difficulty resting, chronic pressure, emotional
exhaustion.

**Goals.** Feel calm. Enjoy success. Stop overthinking. Feel safe. Improve
relationships. Increase capacity. Feel like herself again.

**Entry points.** Instagram, podcast, LinkedIn, Google search, referral,
webinar.

**Products.** Capacity Assessment → free webinar → Life Beyond Trauma →
Membership.

**Success.** She no longer survives success — she enjoys it.

**Customer journey:**

```
Instagram → reads article → takes Capacity Assessment
  → receives personalised results → email sequence → registers for webinar
  → books consultation → joins Life Beyond Trauma → Monthly Membership
  → long-term Institute community
```

## Persona 02 — The Practitioner (secondary)

**Context.** Age 28–60; therapist, counsellor, coach, psychologist, health
professional.

**Pain points.** Needs structured frameworks, wants better client outcomes,
feels overwhelmed by fragmented training, seeks credibility.

**Goals.** Develop expertise. Gain certification. Learn evidence-informed
methods. Join a respected professional community.

**Entry points.** Conference, LinkedIn, podcast, research article,
professional referral.

**Products.** Academy → Certification → Practitioner Community → Faculty
Development.

**Success.** Becomes a certified TNSI practitioner, continues learning
through the Institute, may eventually teach or supervise.

## Persona 03 — Executive Leader (third)

**Context.** Age 35–60; founder, CEO, executive, senior leader.

**Pain points.** Decision fatigue, constant responsibility, leadership
pressure, emotional load, burnout, poor recovery.

**Goals.** Think clearly. Lead effectively. Increase capacity. Improve
resilience. Protect health. Build sustainable performance.

**Entry points.** LinkedIn, speaking event, referral, podcast, executive
article.

**Products.** Leadership Assessment → Executive Advisory → Leadership
Intensive → Corporate Programmes.

**Success.** Leads with greater clarity, regulation, and sustainable
capacity.

## Persona 04 — The Curious Learner

**Context.** Discovering nervous system education for the first time; not
yet ready for a programme; searching for understanding.

**Needs.** Reliable information, simple explanations, trust, education.

**Products.** Insights, newsletter, podcast, assessment, webinars.

**Success.** Develops trust in TNSI and gradually progresses into deeper
learning.

## User decision journey (applies across all personas)

| Stage            | Questions                                                                          | Platform response                                                             |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Awareness     | "Why do I feel like this?" "Why can't I switch off?" "Is something wrong with me?" | Instagram, articles, podcast, SEO, YouTube                                    |
| 2. Understanding | "Why does this happen?" "Can this change?"                                         | Assessment, research, frameworks, webinar, newsletter                         |
| 3. Trust         | "Can Caroline help me?" "Does this approach make sense?"                           | Testimonials, founder story, case studies, programme info, free learning      |
| 4. Commitment    | "Am I ready?" "What programme fits me?"                                            | Consultation, assessment results, application, programme comparison           |
| 5. Growth        | "What's next?"                                                                     | Membership, advanced learning, Academy, Executive Advisory, community, events |

## Emotional journey

```
Uncertainty → understanding → relief → trust → commitment
  → transformation → advocacy
```

This progression should guide every interaction across the platform — see
[06-feature-specification.md](./06-feature-specification.md)'s product
design principles, which operationalize this into per-feature questions.

## Customer journey principles

Every user should: feel understood within 30 seconds; understand the
Institute within 60 seconds; know which pathway fits them within 3
minutes; take a meaningful next step before leaving the platform.

## Success metrics by persona

- **High-Achieving Women:** Assessment → Consultation → Life Beyond Trauma.
- **Practitioners:** Research → Academy → Certification.
- **Executives:** Leadership Content → Advisory → Corporate Engagement.
- **Learners:** Content → Newsletter → Webinar → Assessment.

## Design principle (carries into product/feature work)

Every page, feature, automation, email, and AI interaction should answer
one question: **"What is the next best step for this specific person?"**
The platform should never treat every visitor the same — personalization
by persona/stage is a product requirement, not a nice-to-have.
