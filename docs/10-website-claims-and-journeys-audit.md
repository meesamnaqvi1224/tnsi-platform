# 10 — Public Website Claims and Customer Journey Audit (W-02)

**Audit date:** 23 October 2026 · **Baseline:** production `main` at `400535a` (includes PR #8; **excludes** W-01 / PR #9 and the membership branch) · **Method:** source review of `apps/web`, `packages/*`, `content/*`; read-only fetches of the live site (`tnsi-platform.vercel.app`, `thenervoussysteminstitute.com`); Notion decisions register and product-spec pages; the `feat/membership-trial-policy` branch read through Git only.

This is an audit and plan. **No code, CMS content, environment variable, deployment, booking, form submission or email was changed or triggered.** Findings are tagged **Verified** (reproduced in source and/or on the live site), **Partially verified**, **Unverified** (depends on a dashboard or fact not inspectable here), **Owner decision**, or **Legal review**. Earlier audit history (Notion “Product & Delivery Status”, “Decisions & Approvals Register”, `docs/membership-commercial-decisions.md`, `docs/09-launch-checklist.md`) is unchanged and remains the record for those topics.

## 1. Executive summary

- **No P0 found.** No secret, credential or personal-data exposure was found in the public surface reviewed.
- **Seven P1s.** The most important, because it is live now: **Google Analytics loads on every page before any consent** on production (`gtag.js?id=G-233HWVPKCK` is in the live homepage HTML). W-01 / PR #9 fixes this (and fails closed until a measurement ID is set), but is unmerged.
- **The public site is mostly honest and well-hedged** (Method, Executive Advisory, Return Path copy and the prospectus pages say plainly what is not available). The material risks are concentrated in five places: **articles**, **Practitioner Certification claims**, **research claims**, **the assessment’s “we’ll email you” promise**, and **billing UI on production code**.
- **No outbound email is implemented anywhere in the repository** (no Resend/SendGrid/SMTP code). Every “we’ll send you…” promise therefore depends on Flowi workflows/notifications, which the owner has said are not built yet. Confirmation screens prove nothing about delivery.
- **Smallest safe fixes first:** (1) merge the W-01 technical commits; (2) stop serving placeholder/mismatched articles; (3) align five pieces of copy with reality (assessment email promise, discovery-call length, “available today”, “16-module”, research status lines). None needs a redesign.
