# 03 — Business Model

> Status: Drafted (v1.0), source: founding Business Model & Product
> Ecosystem doc.

## Business philosophy

TNSI is not built around a single programme — it's built around a lifelong
learning ecosystem. Every product serves a specific stage of the client's
journey while reinforcing the Institute's mission. The goal is not simply
to sell courses; it's to create meaningful, long-term transformation
through education, structured pathways, and trusted relationships.

## The TNSI ecosystem (four divisions)

- **TNSI Programs** — for high-achieving women seeking personal
  transformation.
- **TNSI Advisory** — for founders, executives, and leaders seeking greater
  capacity and sustainable performance.
- **TNSI Academy** — for therapists, practitioners, and professionals
  pursuing structured training and certification.
- **TNSI Insights** — the public knowledge hub: articles, frameworks,
  research, podcasts, webinars, thought leadership.

Each division supports the others while serving a distinct audience.

## Customer lifecycle: four entry points

1. **Educational content** (articles, Instagram, LinkedIn, podcast,
   YouTube, guest appearances) — goal: build trust and awareness.
2. **Assessment** (the Capacity Assessment™ — personal capacity profile,
   nervous system insights, recommended learning path) — goal:
   qualification and segmentation.
3. **Live learning** (free webinars, masterclasses, workshops) — goal:
   education and relationship building.
4. **Direct referral** — visitor arrives ready to enquire about Life
   Beyond Trauma, Executive Advisory, or the Academy directly.

## Revenue model — the product ladder

| Stage                        | Purpose                      | Products                                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Awareness (free)             | Education and trust          | Instagram, LinkedIn, podcast, Insights, newsletter, Capacity Assessment, free webinar |
| Engagement (low commitment)  | Develop confidence           | Monthly Membership, digital workshops, live events                                    |
| Transformation (core)        | Deep personal transformation | Life Beyond Trauma Method (12-module flagship)                                        |
| Leadership (premium)         | High-level support           | Private Executive Advisory, corporate consulting, leadership intensives               |
| Professional (certification) | Professional education       | Practitioner training, certification, faculty development, practitioner registry      |

## Revenue streams

**Primary:** Life Beyond Trauma, Membership, Executive Advisory, Academy
training, certification, corporate programmes.

**Secondary:** live events, conferences, books, digital resources, speaking
engagements, licensing.

**Future:** mobile app, subscription platform, AI premium membership,
enterprise partnerships, research collaborations.

## Customer journey (full arc)

```
Awareness → Assessment → Newsletter → Webinar → Consultation
  → Life Beyond Trauma → Membership → Advanced Learning
  → Academy or Advisory → Long-term Community
```

The goal is a relationship measured in years, not weeks.

## Membership strategy

Membership is not a separate business — it's the connective tissue of the
Institute. Purpose: maintain regulation, continue learning, strengthen
community, support long-term integration. Includes monthly teachings, Q&A
sessions, a practice library, guest experts, community, and early access to
Institute resources.

## Executive advisory model

Positioned explicitly as nervous system advisory, not coaching. Focus
areas: leadership capacity, decision-making, performance under pressure,
emotional regulation, strategic thinking, sustainable leadership.
Engagement formats: private advisory, executive intensives, corporate
consulting, leadership retreats.

## Academy growth model

```
Life Beyond Trauma → Practitioner Training → Certification
  → Registry → Faculty → Research Collaboration
```

The Academy creates both impact and recurring professional engagement.

## Intellectual property strategy

Rather than relying on generic industry language, TNSI develops
proprietary, trademarked frameworks that strengthen brand recognition and
become the foundation for education, marketing, certification, and
advisory: **Capacity Recalibration Model™**, **The Overfunctioning
Cycle™**, **Leadership Capacity Framework™**, **Pressure-to-Performance
Model™**, **Capacity Compass™**.

## Community strategy

The community exists to deepen learning, not simply increase engagement.
Areas: High-Achieving Women, Members, Programme Alumni, Practitioners,
Leaders, Faculty, Research Discussions.

## AI integration strategy

AI extends the Institute — it does not replace human expertise.
Applications: Capacity Assessment support, learning recommendations,
resource discovery, programme navigation, practice guidance, content
search, reflection prompts. A future AI Institute Guide may be trained
exclusively on TNSI frameworks and educational materials. See
[07-technical-architecture.md](./07-technical-architecture.md) for the AI
risk callout — this is a product/business decision with real legal
exposure given the subject matter, not just an engineering one.

## Go-to-market

Driven by the customer lifecycle above: organic content (Instagram,
LinkedIn, podcast, YouTube) → Capacity Assessment as the qualification
mechanism → webinars/masterclasses → consultation → paid product. CRM
(Flowi) automation drives nurture sequences and re-engagement at each
stage (see [07-technical-architecture.md](./07-technical-architecture.md)).

## Strategic partnerships (future)

Healthcare organisations, universities, leadership institutes, corporate
wellbeing programmes, professional associations, mental health
organisations, global conference partners.

## Business success principles

Every new product must answer three questions — if the answer to any is
no, it should not be built:

1. Does it strengthen the Institute's authority?
2. Does it increase the user's capacity?
3. Does it fit within the long-term ecosystem?

## Long-term success metric

Success is not measured only by programme sales — it's measured by
creating an institution people trust for decades. The website is the
gateway; the ecosystem is the product; the Institute is the brand.

## Key risks

- **Unit economics (CAC, LTV, margins) are not yet documented** — flagged
  here as an open gap, not yet knowable from source material.
- **AI/legal risk**: nervous-system/mental-health-adjacent subject matter
  carries real product and legal risk if AI guidance drifts into
  clinical/diagnostic territory (see
  [07-technical-architecture.md](./07-technical-architecture.md)).
- **Brand dilution risk**: the ecosystem depends on every division
  reinforcing institutional authority rather than competing for attention
  — pricing/packaging decisions should be evaluated against this.
